OFFICIAL COMMUNITY INVITATION

TRIWAVE

A fun, safe, creative community built around growth, unity, positive energy, and opportunities for young people.
OPENING DAY: October 15th, 2026

Hi, I’m President Edwards. I’ve been wanting to show everyone this for a very long time, but I think that it’s time that I do. I am presenting you the new Triwave Community. This community was made to reduce underage smoking and create somewhere kids can go to have fun. But there are some things that everyone has to do in order to experience the Triwave.

The Triwave Expectations

1. Duties

Everyone must obey the laws. People under 18 must not smoke.

2. Responsibilities

Volunteer in community events that help prevent smoking and violence.

3. Traits

People shouldn’t be criticized for their opinions and beliefs. Everyone should be treated with respect.

Community Safety

Additional police presence may be used at certain locations to help keep public spaces safe.

🚭 NO UNDERAGE SMOKING
Triwave is designed to be a safe, welcoming place for young people. No underage smoking is allowed.

Come Have Fun

Fun games will be hosted 3 times a week, along with community events and activities where people can meet, play, create, and connect.

DON’T MISS OUT!!

Triwave officially opens to visitors on October 15th, 2026.

We hope everyone enjoys the Triwave — and maybe one day, we can turn this idea into an actual city.
